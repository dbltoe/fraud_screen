# Fraud Screen v1.1.0 changelog

## Plugins Library "what's new"

Trusted customers (by customer ID, or automatically after a number of completed orders; never guests), an optional billing-vs-delivery name rule, a fuller held-order alert email, and three notifiers for add-ons. New settings install switched off and existing settings are kept on upgrade.

## Full entry

**v1.1.0 (2026-10-29)**

- New rule, off by default: billing and delivery names differ. Set its points under **Configuration → Fraud Screen** if you want it.
- Trusted customers: list customer IDs whose orders are never held, or trust customers automatically after a number of completed orders. Signed-in accounts only; guest checkouts are never trusted.
- The held-order alert email now names the customer and gives their email, the order total and the payment method.
- Three notifiers for add-ons to add rules and decide holds: `NOTIFY_FRAUD_SCREEN_SCORING`, `NOTIFY_FRAUD_SCREEN_BEFORE_HOLD` and `NOTIFY_FRAUD_SCREEN_HELD`. See the readme's developer section.
- The log now says why a scored order wasn't held: `passed (trusted: ...)` or `passed (released by an add-on)`.
- Upgrade in Plugin Manager: the four new settings are added switched off, and every setting you already have keeps its value.
