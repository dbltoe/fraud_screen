**Fraud Screen v1.1.0 is out**

Most of this release came from ideas a store owner sent in on GitHub, thanks to them.

**Trusted customers.** If Fraud Screen keeps holding a customer you know is genuine, such as a dropshipper or a regular who ships to clients, you can now exempt them. List their customer ID numbers under **Configuration > Fraud Screen > Trusted Customers**, or have customers trusted automatically once they have a set number of completed orders. A trusted customer's orders are still scored and logged, just never held. Trust only goes to signed-in accounts, never to an email address and never to a guest checkout (One Page Checkout's shared guest account included), because at guest checkout anyone can type any email address.

**A new rule, off by default:** billing and delivery names differ. Gifts trip it all the time, so if you turn it on, keep it low (15-25) and let it add to the other signals.

**A fuller alert email.** If you've filled in **Notify This Address When An Order Is Held**, the alert now also gives the customer's name and email, the order total and the payment method, so you can judge the order before opening the admin. If you haven't filled it in, that setting is worth a look: it's empty by default, so no alert is sent until you do.

**For developers:** three new notifiers (`NOTIFY_FRAUD_SCREEN_SCORING`, `NOTIFY_FRAUD_SCREEN_BEFORE_HOLD`, `NOTIFY_FRAUD_SCREEN_HELD`) let another plugin add its own rules or release a hold without touching Fraud Screen's files. The readme's developer section lists the parameters.

To upgrade, put the v1.1.0 folder beside your current version in zc_plugins/FraudScreen/ and click **Upgrade** in Admin > Modules > Plugin Manager. The four new settings arrive switched off, and every setting you already have keeps its value. Zen Cart 2.1.0 through 3.0.0.
