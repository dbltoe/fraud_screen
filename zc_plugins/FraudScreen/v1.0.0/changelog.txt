Fraud Screen for Zen Cart

v1.0.0
- Initial release.
- Scores orders after creation against configurable signals: watched telephone
  numbers, email patterns, billing/delivery state and country mismatches,
  watched product models, and repeat use of a phone number or delivery address.
- Holds orders reaching the threshold in a configurable order status and records
  the score and reasons in the order history.
- Optional alert email and optional per-order decision logging.
- Screening never interrupts checkout; a false positive delays an order rather
  than losing the sale.
